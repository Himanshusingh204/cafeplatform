# CONTENT_GUIDE.md

## Tone

Warm, confident, short, human, restaurant-focused.

### Avoid AI-sounding clichés
- ❌ "where culinary excellence meets innovation"
- ❌ "embark on a gastronomic journey"
- ❌ "revolutionizing the dining experience"
- ❌ "elevating every bite"

### Prefer
- Short, concrete sentences about food, ingredients, and place.
- Specific dishes and real flavors (e.g. "Slow-cooked tomato gravy", "Charcoal-grilled paneer").

## Homepage

- Hero: one short, confident line + subline + CTA ("Explore Menu").
- Signature dishes: 3–4 featured items from the DB.
- About preview: 2–3 sentences + link.
- Menu highlights: category-led preview.
- Gallery preview: 4–6 photos.
- Why visit us: 3 concrete reasons (not stats walls).
- Reviews: 3 short, human quotes.
- Location CTA: address, hours, directions + call.

## Menu Copy

- Names: simple and clear ("Butter Chicken", "Paneer Tikka").
- Descriptions: one short sentence on ingredients/taste (≤ 200 chars).
- No price padding or fake discounts unless real.

## Alt Text

Every image gets meaningful alt (e.g. "Butter chicken with cream swirl, garnished with coriander").

## FAQ

Common, honest questions: hours, vegetarian options, spice levels, reservations, catering, dietary notes.

## Legal Pages

Privacy + Terms: plain-language, honest about data collected (contact form) and how it's used. No invented legalese claims.
